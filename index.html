War for the Ember Throne

The old king is dead. Five great houses gather their banners across the realm of Aldmere. Seize the Ember Throne and hold it for three turns — or break every rival house — to claim the crown.

How to play · Tap your holding, then tap a neighbour to march (enemy = battle). Levy troops with gold. Walls make holdings harder to take. Winter halves income and bleeds armies. Drag to pan · scroll / pinch to zoom.
